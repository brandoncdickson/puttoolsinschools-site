About us

Introducing students to the trades.

Put Tools In Schools is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Charlotte, North Carolina.

Our purpose

What we set out to do

01

Introduce careers

Introduce local students to careers in the building, manufacturing, and automotive industries.

02

Learn with real tools

Provide a hands-on educational experience in how to use the tools of various trades.

03

Discover purpose

Inspire students to discover their God-given gifts, that will lead them to a career path that provides purpose and happiness.

Where we show up

Into the middle schools

We take the trades to students through Career Days. Recent visits include Bailey Middle School, where more than 400 8th-grade students took part, and Mint Hill Middle School.

Our Tool Box Work Days bring volunteers together to prepare the tool boxes that connect students to the trades and their tools.

“We must change the image of working with your hands and tools.”

Put Tools In Schools, on a Mint Hill Middle School Career Day
