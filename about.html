About us

Nobody should be waiting for a tool to start learning.

Put Tools In Schools exists to close the gap between what teachers want to teach and what their classrooms can actually do.

Our story

Good ideas shouldn't die in the supply closet.

Every school has teachers with brilliant projects and nothing to build them with. We connect those classrooms with equipment, training, and people who want to help.

Our work is simple: find tools that deserve a second life, match them to the schools that need them, and stay around long enough to make sure they get used.

  • We start with what teachers asked for, not what we happen to have.
  • We pair every delivery with training and setup help.
  • We report where tools go so supporters can see the impact.

What we believe

Three principles we won't bend

Access first

Every school deserves a fair shot, not just the ones with a development office.

Safe by design

Every tool comes with safety basics, and training comes before the first use.

Community powered

Teachers, volunteers, and businesses all have a role. Nobody does this alone.

Want to be part of the story?

Whether you teach, donate, volunteer, or run a business, there is a place for you here.
