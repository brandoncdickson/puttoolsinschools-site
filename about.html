About us

Connecting students to careers in the trades.

We are a volunteer group of individuals and companies in Charlotte, North Carolina. We show young people the career opportunities in the trades, and invite others to get involved in their education.

Our mission

What we set out to do

01

Introduce the trades

Introduce local students to construction, automotive, and manufacturing trades through events at schools and community centers.

02

Hands-on with real tools

Give students hands-on experience with tools, taught by industry professionals.

03

Connect to careers

Connect students with local companies for career opportunities, and inspire them to discover their God-given gifts and a path that brings purpose and happiness.

Our story

From a radio campaign to a full calendar

  1. 2018

    It starts on the radio

    Ray Terry, a 54-year veteran of residential construction and host of About Your House Radio for 34 years, launches an awareness campaign about the labor shortage and the image of the trades. The radio show becomes its voice.

  2. 2019–2020

    The first club and Career Days

    An after-school carpentry club at Eastway Middle School builds and installs birdhouses, then picnic tables. Five Trades Career Days at Butler and East Mecklenburg High Schools bring 5 to 10 companies each, until the pandemic puts everything on hold.

  3. 2021

    A nonprofit is born

    Put Tools In Schools becomes a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

  4. 2022

    Gathering steam

    About 11 events, including a public Trades Career Day with Titan Solar that brought together 16 companies.

  5. 2023

    A board takes shape

    About eight events, including a public day at Compton's Automotive. A formation meeting in August draws 28 people, and the first official board meeting follows in September.

  6. 2024

    Expanding alliances

    Events at Parkwood High School, Northwest School of the Arts, and the Apprenticeship Academy of Union County, plus regular sessions with Center 360 and West Boulevard Ministry.

  7. 2025

    Growing the reach

    Plans to take the mobile program to elementary schools and home school networks, expand into the NC Southwest District 6 counties, and build the S.H.E.D. building competition with Connect For Success.

  8. 2026

    A full fall calendar

    17 events on the fall calendar across Charlotte-area schools, plus Saturday Tool Time sessions at the Tool Box.
