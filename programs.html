Programs

Three programs. One goal.

Get equipment into classrooms, make sure teachers feel ready, and give students time to build something real.

Program 01

Tool Drives

Donated, refurbished, and partner-supplied tools go to schools that asked for them, with the right accessories included.

  • Hand tools, electronics kits, and robotics starter sets
  • Matched to grade level and classroom goals
  • Delivered with a quick setup guide
Request tools

Typical request

Grade levels, number of students, current equipment, and one goal for the year. Most requests take a few minutes to fill out.

What teachers get

Practical sessions, lesson starters, and a go-to contact for questions after the workshop.

Program 02

Teacher Training

Short, hands-on workshops that show teachers how to run new tools safely and turn them into lessons.

  • Live workshops or recorded walkthroughs
  • Safety basics and classroom management tips
  • Lesson plans that line up with standards
Bring training to your school
Program 03

Student Labs

Project days and after-school sessions where students design, build, test, and fix real things.

  • Challenge-based projects with clear goals
  • Mentors from our volunteer network
  • Showcase days so students can share what they built
Volunteer as a mentor

Designed for mixed skill levels

Each lab has a starter challenge, a stretch challenge, and prompts that help students explain their thinking.

Tool catalog

What we can put in your room

Availability changes with donations. Tell us what you need and we will tell you what we can match.

Robotics kits

Starter robots, motors, sensors, and coding boards for beginners through advanced builders.

Electronics

Breadboards, microcontrollers, multimeters, soldering stations, and component kits.

Hand and power tools

Safe-use tools for shop, design, and build classes, with guards and training included.

3D printing

Entry-level printers, filament, and design software for prototype and print projects.

Science sensors

Data loggers, probes, and lab sets for hands-on experiments across subjects.

Coding devices

Tablets, single-board computers, and classroom-ready coding tools for every age.

Questions

Good to know

Does it cost my school anything?

Tool requests are free for eligible schools. Some programs ask for a small commitment, like sharing outcomes or hosting a showcase.

Who can request tools?

Teachers, counselors, and school staff can submit a request. Parents and community groups can ask us about student labs.

How long does it take to get tools?

It depends on what is available. We will tell you a realistic timeline once we review your request.

Let's find your match.

Tell us about your classroom and we will suggest what we can offer.
