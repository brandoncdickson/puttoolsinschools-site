Programs

Bringing the trades to students.

Career Days in schools, a hands-on learning lab, community partnerships, and a radio show that has been spreading the word since 2018.

Program 01

Trades Career Days

We host Trades Career Days at schools and company sites. Each one runs 5 to 6 hours, and industry professionals show students how to use the tools of their trade.

  • Construction, manufacturing, and automotive careers
  • Hands-on tool use, guided by industry professionals
  • Connections to local companies and career opportunities

Recent highlights

Program 02

The Tool Box

The Tool Box is our hands-on learning lab at 2931 Gibbon Rd in Charlotte.

Volunteers keep it ready on Tool Box Work Days. Saturday Tool Time sessions put the tools to work on real jobs, like swapping old fluorescent fixtures for new LED lights.
