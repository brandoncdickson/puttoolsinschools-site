Programs

Bringing the trades to students.

Career Days put the trades in front of students. Tool Box Work Days get the tools ready.

Program 01

Career Days

We visit middle schools to show students what careers in the trades look like and where they can lead.

  • Introduces careers in building, manufacturing, and automotive
  • Hands-on time with real tools
  • Recent visits to Bailey Middle School and Mint Hill Middle School
Bring a Career Day →

Bailey Middle School

More than 400 8th-grade students took part. Many had never heard what the trades could offer them.

Mint Hill Middle School

Students built a copper sculpture from copper pipe donated by Precision Plumbing. The piece was left at the school as a gift.

Volunteers prepare the tools

Tool Box Work Days bring volunteers together to prepare the tool boxes that connect students to the trades and their tools.

Why it matters

Students should see the work

Our purpose is to introduce students to careers in the trades, let them use real tools, and inspire them to find a path that gives them purpose and happiness.

Careers

Building, manufacturing, and automotive careers, shown to students who may not know they exist.

Hands-on

A chance to learn how the tools of various trades are used.

Purpose

Help students discover their God-given gifts and find work that brings purpose and happiness.
