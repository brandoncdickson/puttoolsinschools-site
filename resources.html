Questions

Quick answers

Can parents or community groups request tools?

Yes. Parents and community groups can reach out through the contact page, and we will point you to the right program.

Do you accept used or broken equipment?

We accept working and repairable equipment. If you're not sure, send us a list and we'll tell you what we can use.

How do I know my donation is used well?

We share updates on where tools went and what students built with them. Donors can ask for a report at any time.
